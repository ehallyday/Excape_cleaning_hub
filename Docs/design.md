# Excape Cleaning Hub— Website Design Brief

Reference template: **Cleanzi** (Framer, by Framebite) — closest match to the
requested "Cleanza." Structure below is adapted from it, not copied — every
section is chosen to fix the specific pains identified in the business
audit: no web presence, unaddressed negative reviews, no easy booking path.

## Design Goals
1. Make the 4.8★ rating the first thing a visitor sees — proof before pitch.
2. Give a way to book/request a quote in under 10 seconds, no phone call required.
3. Read as established and trustworthy despite having zero existing web presence.

## Page Sections (in order)

1. **Hero**
   - Headline: outcome-focused, not feature-focused (e.g. "A Spotless Home,
     Booked in Minutes" not "We Offer Cleaning Services").
   - Subhead: service area callout — "Serving Port Harcourt homes since [year]."
   - Primary CTA: "Get a Free Quote" (form or WhatsApp link — WhatsApp is
     likely the actual booking channel already in use, don't fight that habit).
   - Secondary CTA: "Call Now" (tel: link, thumb-reachable on mobile).
   - Trust strip directly under the fold: ★ 4.8/5 Google rating + review count,
     shown as a real widget/badge, not a claim in body text.

2. **Services**
   - Two clear tracks, since the business spans both: **Residential Cleaning**
     and **Fumigation**. Card-based, 2–3 cards per track, icon + one-line
     description + "From ₦X" if pricing is public, else "Get Quote."
   - Keep language plain — "Deep Clean," "Move-In/Move-Out," "Pest & Fumigation"
     — avoid agency jargon.

3. **Why Us / Credibility**
   - 3–4 short trust statements: years in business, insured/bonded if true,
     number of homes serviced, response time promise.
   - This section exists specifically to counter the "poor service" reviews —
     don't ignore them, out-signal them with concrete specifics.

4. **Process**
   - 3-step visual: Book → We Clean → You Inspect (or similar). Reduces
     booking anxiety for first-time customers who've seen the mixed reviews.

5. **Testimonials**
   - Pull the *best* real Google reviews verbatim, with reviewer first name.
   - If available, show total review count and average rating again here as
     reinforcement, not just in the hero.
   - Do NOT fabricate quotes — only use verifiable existing reviews.

6. **FAQ**
   - Preempt objections: "What if I'm not satisfied?", "Do you bring your own
     supplies?", "How do I pay?", "Do you serve my area of Port Harcourt?"

7. **Final CTA**
   - Repeat quote form / WhatsApp / call CTA. This is the highest-intent exit
     point — keep it visually identical to the hero CTA for recognition.

8. **Footer**
   - Contact details, service area list, business hours, social links (if any),
     copyright. Full NAP (Name, Address, Phone) for local SEO consistency
     with the Google Business Profile.

## Color Palette
- Base: clean neutral (off-white / light gray background) — reinforces
  "spotless" without being sterile.
- Primary accent: a saturated blue (nods to "Sapphire") — used for CTAs,
  links, icon fills. One primary accent only.
- Secondary accent: a soft teal or green for trust/success cues (checkmarks,
  badges, star ratings) — keep separate from the primary CTA color so buttons
  never compete visually with rating badges.
- Avoid red/orange except for rare urgency callouts (e.g. limited slots) —
  competes with warning connotations given the review-trust issue.

## Typography
- Headings: a clean geometric sans-serif, medium-bold weight — confident,
  not shouty.
- Body: a highly legible sans-serif at 16px+ base size — many visitors will
  be on mobile data connections in Port Harcourt, so favor system fonts or a
  single lightweight webfont to keep load times low.
- Avoid script/decorative fonts — this is a trust-rebuilding site, not a
  boutique brand.

## Imagery
- Real photos over stock where possible: actual team, actual jobs, actual
  Port Harcourt homes. If none exist yet, budget for a single photo session
  before launch — this matters more than any other design decision here.
- If stock is unavoidable short-term, choose diverse, local-feeling imagery —
  avoid obviously Western/generic "cleaning stock photo" sets, they undercut
  the local-trust goal.
- Before/after shots (if available) are the highest-converting content type
  for this niche — prioritize in Services and Testimonials sections.

## Components / Interaction Notes
- Sticky mobile CTA bar (Call + WhatsApp) — most traffic will be mobile;
  don't make users scroll back to the hero to convert.
- Star rating badge should link out to the live Google reviews page, not just
  display a static number — verifiable trust beats claimed trust.
- Forms: minimal fields (name, phone, service needed, area) — every extra
  field is a dropped lead in a market where WhatsApp/phone is the norm.
- Page weight: keep it light — optimize images, avoid heavy animation
  libraries. Connection speed in the target market is a real constraint.

## Explicitly Out of Scope for v1
- Blog/content section (Cleanzi includes one; skip for launch — no proven
  need yet, adds maintenance burden for a business with no existing content
  team).
- Online payment integration — quote-first, pay-on-service is the norm here;
  revisit post-launch if demand shows otherwise.
